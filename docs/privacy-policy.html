Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 23, 2026

Executive Summary: MathX is a privacy-first application. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data. All information remains exclusively on your local device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to MathX ("the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the App.

2. No Data Collection

We built MathX to be completely private. As a result:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • No Location Tracking: The App does not track your location.
  • No Analytics: We do not use third-party tracking tools or cookies to monitor your behavior.
  • No Accounts: You do not need to create an account to use the App.

3. Local Storage Only

All data you input or generate in the App—including game progress, streaks, stats, and settings—is stored locally on your device using browser-based storage mechanisms.

  • This data never leaves your device.
  • We do not have a central database or server that stores your information.
  • If you delete the App's data from your device settings or clear your browser cache, your progress will be permanently removed.

4. Third-Party Services

MathX does not integrate with any third-party advertising networks or data brokers. The App is a 100% ad-free experience designed solely to help you master mental math.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect contact information, we cannot notify you individually. We recommend checking the App's Settings or Info page for links to the most current version of this policy.
